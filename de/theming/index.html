Appearance: themes, accent, department colours, sidebar, icons, logos

Dieser Inhalt ist noch nicht in deiner Sprache verfügbar.

HyperLevel’s look is data. A person picks it under Einstellungen → Darstellung, the agency sets defaults (and locks) under Agentur → White-Label, and nothing in a page component knows about any of it: everything goes through CSS custom properties and a handful of attributes on <html>.

What Where it lives
Choices and validation (shared by server and browser) server/services/appearance-model.ts
Preferences API, agency defaults/locks, logo upload server/modules/appearance.ts, server/services/svg-safe.ts
Colour maths (WCAG contrast, OKLCH, accent derivation) web/src/theme/color.ts
Theme data web/src/theme/themes.ts
Department colours and the route → department map web/src/theme/depts.ts
CSS variable generation web/src/theme/css.ts
Store, cache, apply web/src/theme/store.ts, web/public/theme-boot.js
All styling that uses the variables web/src/theme/theme.css
Sidebar (logo, rail, flyouts, hidden entries) web/src/theme/Sidebar.tsx
Pages web/src/theme/Appearance.tsx, Profiles.tsx, BrandingLook.tsx
Hand-made icon pack web/src/theme/packs/solid.ts
Raum-Modus (tokens, stylesheet, detection) web/src/xr/*, xr.md
Own order of tiles and cards (rules, drag & drop, store) server/services/tile-order.ts, web/src/sortable/* – Kacheln sortieren
Tests tests/theme.test.ts, tests/appearance.test.ts, tests/xr-spatial.test.ts, tests/tile-order.test.ts

What people can choose

Section titled “What people can choose”

Per user (stored on the server, so it follows the account to every device; localStorage only makes the first paint fast):

  • Theme (19, each with a matched light and dark variant) and mode (hell / dunkel / wie das System).
  • Accent colour: any colour (picker or preset). Hover, soft tint, link text, focus ring and the text on the accent (black or white) are derived automatically and stay readable.
  • Bereichsfarben: every area of the menu has its own hue. Intensity Aus / Dezent / Klar / Vibrant, three palettes (Harmonisch, Regenbogen, Gedeckt), and an optional switch that also tints buttons, links and tabs on a department’s pages.
  • Sidebar: full or icons only (Ctrl/⌘+B). On phones the drawer stays.
  • Icon pack: Linien, Fein, Kräftig, Eckig, Duotone, Gefüllt.
  • Density (kompakt / normal / komfortabel) and font size (klein / normal / groß).
  • Raum-Modus (Automatisch / An / Aus): the spatial UI for headsets and display glasses – large targets, glass surfaces, a type scale for eyes + pinch. Its colours are theme tokens (--sp-*, web/src/xr/tokens.ts, checked for contrast in every theme) and its stylesheet loads only while data-spatial="1" is on the root; the store then also sets data-density="comfortable". Never locked by an agency. See xr.md.
  • Visible areas: hide menu entries you do not use, or pick a starter profile.
  • Own order of tiles and cards on every page – drag them where you want them, see Kacheln sortieren.

Hidden areas are a view, not a permission

Section titled “Hidden areas are a view, not a permission”

Hiding an area removes it from the menu, the search box, Ctrl+K and the dashboard widgets. It does not change what the account may do: the permission areas (server/core/permissions.ts) are untouched, the page and its API stay reachable by address, and Alle Bereiche anzeigen at the bottom of the menu brings everything back for the session. Never use it to keep something from a person – use the area permissions of the login for that.

Starter profiles (PROFILES in the model): Agentur, Coach & Kurse, Lokales Business / Handwerk, E-Commerce, Vertriebsteam, Alles. Choosing one shows which areas stay visible before it is applied. New accounts are offered the choice once, right after their first sign-in (accounts younger than 14 days that did not answer yet), and it is available in the Akademie and on the Darstellung page at any time.

Einfach-Modus

Section titled “Einfach-Modus”

Simple by default, powerful on demand. The rules are pure data in server/services/areas-model.ts, shared by the server and the web app; the web side lives in web/src/areas/.

  • Goal setup “Was willst du tun?” – a new login picks one or more goals: Leads & Vertrieb, Kurse & Community, Agentur, Rechnungen & Büro or Alles. Skipping (✕, Esc, “Später”) applies Leads & Vertrieb. Existing logins see a dismissible banner once (“Neu: Einfach-Modus”) with “Ziele wählen” and “Alles behalten”. The goals can be changed at any time in the first card of Einstellungen → Darstellung. An agency that sets goals in its defaults (PATCH /api/agency/branding/look) decides for its clients, who then skip the setup; the lock Sichtbare Bereiche covers the goals too.

  • Adaptive sidebar – with goals chosen the menu shows at most eleven core entries: Dashboard, Konversationen, Kontakte, Kalender, Aufgaben, Akademie (so new logins always find the guided tours) plus the areas of the goals. Everything else is under Mehr, a searchable panel with a one-line description per area, where areas can be pinned into the menu or moved under “Mehr”. Einstellungen stays pinned at the bottom. Alles (or no goals) keeps the classic full menu. Hidden areas and permissions still apply on top.

  • Promotion by use – each visit of an area is counted per person (not per sub-account). An area under “Mehr” opened at least three times in 14 days moves into the menu at the next session start, never while someone works; the counts are read once per browser session. Areas the person moved under “Mehr” themselves are never promoted.

  • Profi-Funktionen – PRO_FEATURES names expert fields (invoice details, website pro tools, team inbox views, contact pro tools) that are hidden in Einfach-Modus until switched on; the preference is pro. Outside Einfach-Modus nothing is hidden.

  • Page heads – one primary action stays in view. On a phone everything else goes into a “⋯” menu, on wider screens what does not fit into three buttons; destructive actions (.danger) always go into “⋯”. A page opened with ?new=1 starts its primary action once (quick actions).

  • Forms and tables (web/src/areas/fields.tsx, rules in fields-model.ts) – in Einfach-Modus a form shows only what most people fill in. Secondary fields go into <MoreFields id="…" values={[…]}>, one “Weitere Felder” toggle that opens by itself when one of its fields already holds a value and stays open once someone opened it (per form, in this browser). Expert settings go into <ProOnly>. Tables pick their columns with useSimpleColumns(id, cols, essential): the essential columns plus those switched on in the small “Spalten” picker. In Profi everything shows inline, nothing is hidden. New forms follow the same rule: required and everyday fields visible, the rest behind the toggle. A guided tour whose target sits in a folded section unfolds it by itself.

  • Einstellungen and Agentur (rules in web/src/areas/settings-nav.ts) – the tab bar of Einstellungen shows Firma, E-Mail & SMS, KI-Anbieter, Team, Abo, Darstellung and Mein Konto plus the sections of the chosen goals (Leads: Kanäle, Live-Chat; Kurse and Rechnungen & Büro: Rechnungen, Stripe & Zahlungen; Agentur: Sub-Accounts, Kanäle). Everything else is one click away under Weitere Einstellungen: a small dialog, grouped (Nachrichten & Kanäle, Rechnungen & Zahlungen, KI, Service, Daten/Recht/Schnittstellen, Konto & Team) with a search that also knows what people type (SMTP, IBAN, DKIM, Webhook, DSGVO, 2FA …). Agentur works the same way with Weitere Bereiche (Projekte, Kunden, SaaS, Snapshots, Domains, White-Label in the bar). The open section always stays in the bar, so links and tours keep working; Profi shows every tab as before. On the Firma tab, Was muss ich einrichten? lists only what is still missing (company details, e-mail sending, AI key, two-factor login for admins; invoice details and online payment for the goals that sell) – each a link to the section that fixes it; ✕ hides it in this browser. Inside the sections, technical settings (SMTP port and reply-to, tracking, inbound webhook URLs, webhook signing, manual Twilio IDs, SSO claims and scopes, number ranges, e-invoice options, limits, retention, Double-Opt-in texts …) sit behind “Weitere Felder”; a provider’s webhook steps open by themselves when its key is saved but the webhook is still missing.

  • Daily pages (lists and rules in web/src/areas/easy-core.ts, tested in tests/easy-core.test.ts) – what a busy admin sees at first glance in Einfach-Modus; Profi is unchanged:

    • Dashboard: four tiles that call for action – open tasks (overdue in red), unread messages, upcoming appointments, open pipeline – and the next appointments. New contacts, won deals, bookings, forms, messages, revenue, reviews, charts, pipelines, lead sources, plugin widgets and the period picker sit behind one “Mehr anzeigen”.
    • Kontakte: columns Name, E-Mail, Telefon, Tags (Lead-Score, Quelle, Health, Angelegt via “Spalten”; an open smart list brings its own columns, chosen in both modes – docs/sales.md#smart-lists); attribution, company, lead-score and health filters behind “Weitere Filter (n)”; workflow and “Nicht stören” in the bulk bar behind “Mehr”. New contact: name, e-mail, phone, company (website, tags folded). Editing: name, e-mail, phone, company (address, birthday, source and custom fields folded). Contact page: workflows & sequences and the lead score, service, courses and origin panels each behind one toggle; no “Termine” card when there are none.
    • Firmen: new company with name, domain, industry (the rest folded); the list starts with Name, Phase, Ort, Kontakte. Company and record pages show only filled properties; editing shows the required and the first six, the rest folded.
    • Opportunities: the deal dialog shows contact, name, value and stage (currency and expected close folded; open when set); line items, quotes & call notes and the deal properties fold away unless the deal has line items or properties. The deal list starts with Name, Kontakt, Stufe, Wert, Erwarteter Abschluss; the pipeline editor folds the loss reasons.
    • Eigene Objekte: a new record shows required, primary and the first six properties; the list starts with the primary property plus three.
    • Kalender: the booking dialog shows calendar, date, time and contact search (“Neuen Kontakt anlegen”, note and repetition folded). The calendar settings show the tabs Allgemein, Verfügbarkeit, Bestätigung & Erinnerung, Typ & Team and Sync plus every tab that already holds settings – the rest behind “+ n weitere Bereiche”; on Allgemein only name, description, place, duration and colour (timing, URL path and booking questions folded); the custom confirmation text and the booking limits fold away unless set.
    • Both modes: shorter hints (pipeline stages, loss reasons, inbox subtitle), the composer’s placeholder warning only when one is typed, no “Keine Zahlung” section in the appointment drawer, no empty separator under contact data.

  • Marketing & automation (rules in web/src/areas/marketing-fields.ts, tests in tests/easy-marketing.test.ts) – the editors below keep their everyday fields in view and fold the rest; a folded part opens by itself when it differs from what a new item starts with:

    • Kampagnen: sender name and raw HTML, segment and any/without tags, A/B test & follow-up, send time per contact & speed (email-marketing); E-Mail-Designer: the look of blocks, sections and the mail (Darstellung).
    • Workflows: per step its name, error handling and fine-tuning; generated forms show required fields plus five (workflow-actions); the workflow’s error handling.
    • Formulare: placeholder, key, show-if and jumps of a field; description, button, redirect, opportunity, consent, ticket and spam protection of the form.
    • Websites: font, favicon, sharing details, noindex, footer, chat (the <head> code only in Profi unless it holds code); Blog: address, excerpt, author, tags and SEO & Social Media.
    • Sequenzen: description, send window and stop rules; Quizze: intro and rules, points and explanation per question; NPS-Umfragen: question text and smart list.
    • Webinare: photo and address, further kinds of dates/time zone/blackout days/seats, headline/button/redirect and the behaviour tags; Kurse: hosted video, chapters, duration, drip and module of a lesson, access details, discussion & reviews; Community: moderation and points & levels.
    • Ads: in the assistant interests and own audiences, negative keywords, UTM switch, display paths; name, time window and minimum spend of a rule; headline, own questions and thank-you page of a lead form. Bewertungen: the widget code.

New preferences (PATCH /api/appearance): goals, goal_asked, pinned, unpinned, pro. GET /api/appearance adds offer_goals (the banner); offer_profiles now shows the goal setup.

Kacheln sortieren

Section titled “Kacheln sortieren”

Every row of key figures and every grid of cards can be put into your own order: the dashboard (key figures, cards, plugin widgets – also in Einfach-Modus and behind Mehr anzeigen), Agentur → Projekte, the project’s key figures, the key figures of the other areas (helpdesk, calls, accounting, SEPA, shop, reviews, courses, community, sales, cloud …), the lists of cards (courses, communities, agents, boards, objects, project planning, budgets, NPS surveys, AI Studio, integrations) and the Fokus-Modus tiles.

  • Mouse / pen: press anywhere on a tile and move it – there are no handles. The drag starts after 6 px, so a click on the tile, a link or a button in it stays a click. Text fields, selects, sliders, code and tables inside a tile (so their text can be selected and copied), video, audio and canvases never start a drag. To select other text in a tile: double-click a word (triple-click a paragraph), or hold Alt while dragging over it.
  • Touch: press and hold a tile for a moment (350 ms), then move it; a quick swipe still scrolls the page, and a long press let go without moving is still a tap (the card, link or button opens).
  • While dragging, the tile lifts and follows the pointer, the others make room, a dashed outline shows where it lands, and the page scrolls when you get near the top or bottom edge. Esc puts everything back – letting go afterwards is no click.
  • Keyboard: tiles that hold nothing focusable (key figures) share one tab stop per grid; the arrow keys move the focus from tile to tile. On a focused tile (or anything inside it) Alt+Umschalt+←/→ moves it one place, ↑/↓ one row, Pos1/Ende to the start or end – the focus stays on it. Screen readers hear “Verschoben auf Position 3 von 8”. prefers-reduced-motion turns the gliding off.
  • Reihenfolge zurücksetzen – a small link under a grid that only appears once you changed its order.

The order is saved per login and grid in the appearance preferences (tile_order: { [gridId]: string[] }), so it follows you to every device, and in the browser (localStorage, pghl.tile-order:<user> – never in the look cache pghl.look, which everyone on the browser shares), so it shows at once and survives being offline – changes made offline are sent when the connection is back, and a server copy never overwrites them in the meantime. A saved order holds for the rest of the session too: pages, Fokus answers and grids that show up later take it from the browser’s store, never from the copy loaded at sign-in.

It is the same in every sub-account, like the theme: an agency admin who arranges the dashboard’s key figures while switched into one client sees that arrangement in every client. Grids of records (projects, boards, courses …) use the records’ ids as keys, so each sub-account’s records keep their own places within the one list. Tiles that are new appear at the end in their natural order, tiles that are gone drop out. When a grid shows only part of its tiles (a status filter, Einfach-Modus, Mehr anzeigen), the hidden ones keep their place behind their neighbour. It is personal: agency defaults never carry it, no lock applies, and Auf Standard zurücksetzen on the Darstellung page leaves it alone.

PATCH /api/appearance merges it grid by grid: { "tile_order": { "dashboard.stats": ["won", "contacts"] } } changes that grid only, { "dashboard.stats": null } resets it, "tile_order": null resets all. The answer carries it once, in prefs.tile_order (not in effective). Grid ids match [a-z0-9][a-z0-9._:-]{0,79}, keys are 1–120 letters, digits, spaces and _#:./@+=~!&(),'-; at most 200 grids (the app has about 90), 200 keys per grid and 128 KB in all – a body beyond that is answered with 400, and when the stored orders outgrow it the grids changed longest ago go first. When a grid’s list is full, the tiles on screen always stay and the hidden ones ranked last are dropped (deleted records, tiles of earlier Fokus answers). A stored copy that no longer fits the rules loses only the grids or keys that do not fit.

For developers – a grid of direct children:

Children are told apart by their React key (without one: their position in the code, #0, #1 …); pinned={['new']} keeps an “add” tile in place, [data-nodrag] on an element keeps drags from starting there. The grid previews the order with CSS order while dragging (nothing moves in the DOM, iframes do not reload) and renders the new order on drop. Tiles that are not direct children (rows packed from one list, like the Fokus tiles) use useSortable({ id, keys }) and put data-sort-key on each tile. Tiles with live embeds take keepDom (the plugin widgets on the dashboard): the children stay in their natural DOM order and the saved order is shown with CSS order, so a drop or a keyboard move never moves – and reloads – an iframe (the Tab order then follows the natural order). Code that is part of the first download (the dashboard, its plugin widgets) imports SortableGrid from web/src/sortable/lazy.tsx instead: the engine (about 8 KB gzip) is a chunk of its own that starts loading with the app, and the tiles show in their natural order until it is there (a keepDom grid shows nothing until then, so no iframe is mounted twice). Tables, kanban boards, Living Boards and custom report dashboards keep their own arranging.

Agency defaults, locks and per-client overrides

Section titled “Agency defaults, locks and per-client overrides”

Agentur → White-Label has the logos (see below) and Standard-Darstellung der Logins: a default for every setting above and a lock per group (Theme und Hell/Dunkel, Akzentfarbe, Bereichsfarben, Seitenleiste, Icon-Pack, Dichte und Schrift, Sichtbare Bereiche). A locked group cannot be changed by client logins; the server answers 403 (Von deiner Agentur festgelegt: …). Agency admins are never locked. Under Agentur → Kunden → Branding each client can override the defaults and open or close locks (Wie Agentur inherits).

What a person sees = built-in defaults ← agency defaults ← client defaults ← their own choices (for unlocked groups only). Only what a person changed is stored, so later changes of the defaults still reach everyone who did not touch that setting. The agency’s brand colour (color of the branding) is the accent of the standard theme HyperLevel Standard; other themes keep their own accent unless a default or the person picks one.

Logos, marks, favicon, app name

Section titled “Logos, marks, favicon, app name”

The branding has five image slots: logo_url, logo_dark_url, mark_url, mark_dark_url, favicon_url, and show_name (no hides the app name beside the logo). The sidebar picks by its own colour: a dark sidebar takes the “for dark” logo when there is one, a light sidebar (e.g. Papier) the normal one. In the narrow sidebar the square mark is used; without a mark the wide logo is cropped to its left square, without any logo the HyperLevel icon (/icon.svg, PRODUCT_ICON in web/src/theme/brand-icon.ts) – never an initial. The login page takes the “for dark” logo when the page is dark (no wide logo: the mark, else the HyperLevel icon). The favicon falls back to the mark. Clients can have their own set; empty slots fall back to the agency’s.

Uploads accept PNG, JPEG, WebP and SVG (raster ≤ 1 MB, SVG ≤ 256 KB). Raster files must really be what they claim (server/core/files.ts). An SVG is refused, not repaired, if it contains a script, an event handler (onload=…), a javascript: address, <foreignObject>, <iframe>, animation elements, a DOCTYPE/entities, an @import or any reference to something outside the file. Files are stored with an extension from the checked type and served with nosniff and default-src 'none'. Image addresses typed in must start with https:// (or point to our /uploads/).

Themes are data

Section titled “Themes are data”

A theme is eight colours per mode:

  1. Add the id to THEME_IDS in server/services/appearance-model.ts (the server validates against it; THEMES is typed Record<ThemeId, …>, so a missing or extra entry does not compile).
  2. Add the entry above. A light side gives a light sidebar.
  3. Run npm test: tests/theme.test.ts checks every theme in both modes – text and muted text on the page and on cards ≥ 4.5:1 (the Kontrast theme ≥ 7:1), sidebar text, headings and icons, the accent as a UI element ≥ 3:1, text on the accent, link text and focus ring, the status colours as text and all department colours (see below). Use ensureContrast from color.ts to find a passing value.
  4. Pick an original name; palettes may be inspired by well-known families but are never copies of a trademarked theme.

Applying a theme sets data-theme-id and data-theme="light|dark" on <html> and writes one generated <style id="pghl-theme"> with the variables (--bg --panel --text --muted --line --side* --brand* --d-* and the status colours --green --red --yellow, moved in lightness until they read ≥ 4.5:1 as text on the page and on cards). Filled badges and buttons with white text (Löschen, error toasts, counters) use the fixed --green-fill --red-fill --yellow-fill from styles.css instead. Existing rules in styles.css keep working because they use the same variables; theme.css adds the new ones. The generated CSS is @media screen only, so printing stays light.

No flash of the wrong theme

Section titled “No flash of the wrong theme”

web/public/theme-boot.js is a tiny blocking script in <head>. It reads localStorage["pghl.look"] (the effective preferences plus the generated CSS of both modes, written by the store) and sets the attributes and the <style> before the first paint; mode system is resolved with matchMedia. It is an external file because the app’s Content-Security-Policy allows no inline scripts. The server’s answer replaces the cache after sign-in. The old “Hell/Dunkel” choice of the account page (pghl.theme) is carried over once.

Accent derivation

Section titled “Accent derivation”

deriveAccent(accent, surfaces, dark) (color.ts) returns, for one accent on the current theme:

  • on: black or white, whichever has the higher contrast (always ≥ 4.5:1);
  • hover: the accent moved in OKLCH lightness while on stays ≥ 4.5:1;
  • soft: the accent mixed into the panel (selected rows, tags);
  • text: the accent as link/tab text, moved in lightness until it is ≥ 4.5:1 on the panel, the page and soft;
  • ring: focus ring ≥ 3:1; select: background of selected text.

That is why a yellow accent gives yellow buttons with black text and dark amber links.

Department colours

Section titled “Department colours”

web/src/theme/depts.ts: DEPTS (12 departments with a base hue), DEPT_OF_ROUTE (menu route → department; tests/theme.test.ts fails if a menu entry has none) and deptColors(palette, dark, accent, surfaces). Per department the generator emits --d-<id> / --d-<id>-2 (gradient ends, chosen so the ink reads on both), --d-<id>-side (≥ 3.5:1 on the sidebar), --d-<id>-text (≥ 4.5:1 on the page) and --d-<id>-ink (black or white). The intensity is pure CSS in theme.css keyed on html[data-dept-mode]; the element that carries data-dept="crm" (a menu link, .main) gets the variables. To add a department: a DEPTS entry, the routes in DEPT_OF_ROUTE, and one [data-dept='…'] line in theme.css (the test checks all three), plus an offset in HARMONIC for the harmonic palette.

Sidebar rail

Section titled “Sidebar rail”

html[data-sidebar="rail"] narrows the grid column to 68 px (desktop only, min-width: 861px); Sidebar.tsx renders tooltips (a fixed element, because the sidebar scrolls) and opens sub-menus (custom objects under Objekte) as a flyout on hover and focus. Ctrl/⌘+B toggles it (not inside rich-text fields); the choice is the preference sidebar.

Icon packs

Section titled “Icon packs”

Icon (web/src/icons.tsx) is the only way pages draw icons. Four packs are the same Lucide icons drawn by CSS (theme.css, html[data-icons]): fine (thin), bold (thick, round), square (miter joins, crisp edges), duotone (a soft fill in the department colour). Gefüllt is a hand-made set for the menu icons (web/src/theme/packs/solid.ts, 24×24, currentColor, real holes via fill-rule="evenodd"); it is a separate chunk that loads only when chosen, and icons it does not have keep their Lucide drawing.

To add a pack: add its id to ICON_PACKS (model), then either CSS rules under html[data-icons='<id>'] or a pack file exporting Partial<Record<IconName, string>> registered with registerIconPack (load it lazily in store.ts like solid). Add the card text in Appearance.tsx. tests/theme.test.ts checks that a pack file covers every menu icon and contains plain markup only.

Android app

Section titled “Android app”

The Android app is a WebView on the same pages: it follows theme, accent, dark mode and the logo automatically (the status bar colour is the theme’s sidebar colour through <meta name="theme-color">).

Screenshots

Section titled “Screenshots”

Headless Chromium against the seeded demo (npm run screenshots renders the README images). For theme work: sign in as the demo user, PATCH /api/appearance with the look to check, load a page and screenshot – the repository’s scripts/screenshots.mjs shows the setup.
