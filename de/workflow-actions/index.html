Workflow actions and triggers

Dieser Inhalt ist noch nicht in deiner Sprache verfügbar.

HyperLevel workflows start at a trigger, run through steps (actions, waits, branches) and can be tested, versioned and moved between sub-accounts as JSON. The library is built in – no Composio or Zapier needed for the common work:

  • 130+ actions: formatter, math, a sandboxed code step, find/find-or-create contact, go-to and goal jumps, A/B/n split tests, loops with a safe cap, drip, waits (for a field condition, a time window, a date relative to a field), webhook with retries and response mapping, per-run variables, internal notifications, round-robin assignment, opportunities, tasks, appointments, DND, tags, invoices/payment links/contracts, community, AI steps and 23 native app steps.
  • 140+ triggers with filters on every one of them: field changed (old/new), birthday/anniversary, cron schedule, form abandoned, page visited, payment failed/refunded, subscription ended, no-show/rescheduled, task overdue, unanswered conversation, review rating, custom events from the API, and the older ones (tags, forms, deals, messages …).

The exact numbers are computed from the registries (GET /api/workflow-catalog → counts); tests/wfx-counts.test.ts fails if either README promises more than exists, and tests/docs.test.ts fails when a type is missing from All actions or All triggers – npm run docs:gen adds the missing rows.

The editor

Section titled “The editor”

Automationen → open a workflow.

  • + Schritt opens the action picker: categories on the left (with counts), search on top (name, description and keywords; ↑/↓ and Enter work without a mouse), and a documentation pane on the right that says what the action does, what it needs (for example a connection under Apps → Direkt-Verbindungen) and which variables it sets. Double-click or + Hinzufügen inserts it.
  • Steps that have no editor of their own get a generated form from the catalogue: text, numbers, selects, tags, weekdays, conditions, lists of rows, pickers for pipelines, stages, calendars, forms, team members, products, contract templates, community groups and social channels, a Ergebnis speichern in target (variable or contact field) and a code field with the function reference. Fields appear only while they matter (showIf). Beispiel einfügen fills a working example.
  • Every step can get a name (shown in the flow and in the log) and has a collapsible documentation under its title.
  • Einfach-Modus (theming): a step shows its action fields; its name, Fehler ignorieren und weiter and the fine-tuning sit behind Weitere Felder – Text ist HTML and Service-Nachricht (e-mail, SMS), the expression of Wenn/Sonst and Ziel, name/currency/close date of a new opportunity, the pipeline filter of Opportunity-Status, Anlegen, wenn keine offene existiert, the webhook method. Generated forms show the required fields and at most five others; retries, timeouts, headers, query, auth, response mapping and the like fold away (splitCatalogFields in web/src/areas/marketing-fields.ts). The toggle opens by itself when one of those fields differs from its default. Einstellungen → Fehlerbehandlung folds the same way. Profi shows everything.
  • Containers hold steps of their own: Wenn/Sonst (Ja/Nein), Split-Test (up to five paths) and Schleife. Moving, removing and finding steps works inside them.
  • The trigger is chosen from a list grouped by topic. Under it: what the trigger does, the data it brings ({{event.…}}), its own settings (for the newer triggers) and Zusätzliche Filter for every trigger.
  • Header buttons: Testlauf, Speichern, Aktivieren/Pausieren. Tabs: Ablauf, Verlauf, Versionen, Einstellungen (with export and delete).
  • Liste | Canvas above the flow switches between the list editor and the visual canvas. Both edit the same steps; the choice is remembered in the browser. Every step card has an on/off switch (pause) and a ⋮ menu: Kopieren, Duplizieren, Verschieben …, Pausieren/Fortsetzen, Löschen (copy and paste).

Every dialog has a ✕ at the top right, closes with Esc, and puts the focus back where it was.

The canvas

Section titled “The canvas”

Ablauf → Canvas shows the workflow as a diagram, like HighLevel’s Advanced Builder – the same step JSON as the list, so a change in one editor is in the other at once.

  • Picture: a dotted grid; white node cards with a coloured icon tile (colour = category), the step’s name or summary, an on/off switch and a ⋮ menu; the trigger on top (a dashed Auslöser hinzufügen card when a workflow has none); header cards for the lanes of Wenn/Sonst (Ja/Nein), Split-Test (A · 50 % …) and Schleife (a dashed line goes back to the loop); lanes join again below; END pills where the flow stops; a round + on every connector inserts a step there (with something on the clipboard it offers Einfügen as well).
  • Moving around: drag the background to pan, mouse wheel or two fingers (pinch) to zoom, the zoom stack bottom right (+, percent = back to 100 %, −, fit) and a minimap (click or drag to jump). Keys: ↑/↓ walk the steps in reading order (into lanes and out again), ←/→ jump to the nearest step at the side, Shift+arrows pan, Enter opens a step, + / − / 0 / F zoom and fit, P pauses, Ctrl/⌘+C / V / D copy, paste after the selected step and duplicate, Delete removes, N adds a note, Shift+F10 opens the step’s menu, ? lists them all.
  • Arranging: steps, lane headers, the trigger and notes can be dragged. Positions are stored in settings.layout as offsets from the automatic layout (offsets[<step id> | trigger | <step id>/<lane>] = [dx, dy]): moving a container moves its lanes with it, inserting a step keeps a hand-arranged canvas tidy, and a node without an offset sits where the auto-layout puts it. Anordnen drops all offsets (notes stay). Saved with the workflow like any other change.
  • Sticky notes (Notiz, N, or right-click on the background) are nodes of their own with text, five colours and a size (settings.layout.notes); the engine never reads them.
  • Statistik shows on every step what the existing run counters say – of the last 300 runs: ✓ passed, ⚠ errors, ↷ skipped (paused), ⏳ waiting there now.
  • Settings: clicking a step opens its usual form in a drawer on the right with a sticky footer: Abbrechen discards, Speichern applies and saves the workflow. ✕ and Esc close it (asking first when something changed). Missing settings show on the node (red, with the count) and at the top of the drawer.
  • The canvas is a chunk of its own (web/src/workflow-canvas/WorkflowCanvas.tsx, hand-rolled HTML + SVG, no new dependency), loaded only when someone opens it; animations are off with prefers-reduced-motion. The server keeps settings.layout within limits (800 offsets, 100 notes of 2 000 characters; offsets of deleted steps are dropped – server/modules/workflow-canvas.ts).

Copy and paste

Section titled “Copy and paste”

Steps travel between workflows – also between sub-accounts and browser tabs – through a clipboard in the browser’s local storage (format gohigh-steps, version 1):

  • Kopieren (⋮ menu or Ctrl/⌘+C on the canvas) copies a step with everything inside it. The server cleans the copy like an export: secrets of HTTP steps (authorization headers, passwords) are blanked.
  • Einfügen appears at every insert position (list: next to + Schritt; canvas: on the +) while the clipboard holds steps. Like the import, every step gets a new id, jumps between the pasted steps follow their new ids, a jump to a step that is neither pasted nor in this workflow is cleared, and references to pipelines, forms, calendars, workflows, products, groups, courses, links or templates that do not exist here come back as warnings to fix. Nothing is saved until the workflow is.
  • Duplizieren puts a copy right after the step (new ids, without the clipboard); Verschieben … lets you pick any insert position, also inside another lane (Esc cancels); Löschen asks first.

Pause a step

Section titled “Pause a step”

The switch on a step card (or Pausieren in its menu, P on the canvas) sets disabled: true on the step. The step stays where it is, but the engine skips it: the run log gets the line Übersprungen – Schritt ist pausiert (skipped: true) and the flow goes on with the next step. A paused container (Wenn/Sonst, Split-Test, Schleife) skips its lanes too; a jump onto a paused step continues after it. Test runs show the skipped steps the same way (badge übersprungen (pausiert)). Paused steps are drawn dashed and are not validated.

Split test winner

Section titled “Split test winner”

The step Split-Test has the option Gewinner automatisch übernehmen. While the workflow is active, the server checks it every ten minutes: once every path has at least 100 contacts, the paths together reached at least 10 goals, and the path with the best goal rate is ahead of every other path with statistical significance (two-proportion z-test, p < 0.05), that path gets 100 % and the others 0 %. The decision is stored on the step (auto_winner_result: path, goal rate, p, time) and taken only once; contacts already in a path stay there (“Immer derselbe Pfad”). Without goals in the paths there is nothing to compare – put a Ziel step into each path.

Folders and bulk actions

Section titled “Folders and bulk actions”

Above the workflow list, Ordner anlegen starts folders; a folder can hold sub-folders (up to five levels, + next to the folder row creates one inside the selected folder). Clicking a folder shows its workflows and those of its sub-folders, Ohne Ordner the rest. ✎ renames, ✕ deletes a folder – its workflows and sub-folders move one level up, nothing is lost.

Mehrere bearbeiten opens a list of the workflows shown: tick several and move them into a folder, activate, pause or delete them together. Status changes and deletes run through the normal routes one by one, so each workflow gets the same check as when activated by hand – a workflow that fails it is reported by name, the others go on – and each change lands in the history (undo). Folders travel in snapshots (by name). API: GET/POST /api/workflow-folders, PATCH/DELETE /api/workflow-folders/:id, POST /api/workflows/bulk {action: move|activate|pause|draft|delete, ids, folder_id}; MCP ghl_workflow_folders, ghl_workflow_bulk (activate/delete need a confirmation in the agent).

Validation

Section titled “Validation”

Saving checks the steps (server/services/workflow-validate.ts, the same rules in the browser): fields the catalogue marks as required (only while they are shown), the older steps’ essentials (e-mail without subject and text, SMS or note without text, tags, field, wait duration or date, conditions with field and value, pipeline, status, task title, webhook URL, target workflow, social channels, at least two split-test paths) and jump targets that no longer exist. Issues show on the step in both editors (red, with the message) and – after the first save – update while you fix them. Saving a draft is never refused; Aktivieren with incomplete steps asks first. The answers of GET and PATCH /api/workflows/:id carry issues: [{step, code, field?, params?, message}], and POST /api/workflows/:id/validate checks unsaved steps ({steps}).

Variables and results

Section titled “Variables and results”
  • Each run has its own variables ({{var.name}}; in conditions the field var.name). They survive waits and are stored with the run. Names starting with _ are internal.
  • Most steps have Ergebnis speichern in: a variable (var.total) or a contact field (first_name, custom.budget). Contact fields are written by the engine, and a real change triggers Feld geändert (see Trigger filters for the loop guards).
  • Steps that look something up set well-known variables, shown as chips in the picker (for example var.contact_found, var.found_contact.email, var.deal.value, var.replied, var.discount_code).
  • The event of the trigger is {{event.…}}, the appointment, opportunity or message of the event are {{appointment.…}} etc. Merge fields support a fallback: {{contact.first_name|there}}.

Expression language

Section titled “Expression language”

The steps Code, Rechnen → Eigene Formel, Springe zu, Ziel-Sprung, Warten bis Bedingung, Tags je nach Bedingung, the conditions of Wenn/Sonst and the trigger filters accept a small expression language. It is not JavaScript: a hand-written tokenizer, parser and interpreter (server/services/expr.ts) – no eval, no new Function, no globals, no process/require, no prototypes or constructors, no network, no files. The program sees deep copies of contact, event, var and location and a fixed set of functions.

Examples:

Functions (the editor lists them under Ausdruckssprache: Funktionen und Beispiele; names are lower-case):

abs(zahl), add_business(datum, n), add_business_days(datum, n), add_days(datum, n), add_hours(datum, n), add_months(datum, n), avg(liste), bool(wert), capitalize(text), ceil(zahl), clamp(zahl, min, max), coalesce(a, b, …), concat(a, b, …), contains(text|liste, wert), count_if(liste, x => …), date(wert), diff(von, bis, einheit), diff_days(von, bis), ends_with(text, ende), every(liste, x => …), filter(liste, x => …), find(liste, x => …), first(liste), flatten(liste), floor(zahl), format_currency(zahl, währung?), format_date(datum, muster?, zeitzone?), format_number(zahl, stellen?, sprache?), get(objekt, "pfad.zum.feld", ersatz?), group_count(liste, x => schlüssel), iif(bedingung, dann, sonst), int(wert), is_empty(wert), is_number(wert), is_weekend(datum), join(liste, trenner), json_parse(text), json_stringify(wert), keys(objekt), last(liste), len(text|liste), lower(text), map(liste, x => …), max(a, b, …), min(a, b, …), now(), number(wert), pad_left(text, länge, füllzeichen?), pad_right(text, länge, füllzeichen?), phone(text, vorwahl?), pow(basis, exponent), random(), random_int(von, bis), range(von, bis, schritt?), reduce(liste, (summe, x) => …, start), regex_match(text, muster, gruppe?), regex_replace(text, muster, ersatz), regex_test(text, muster, optionen?), repeat(text, n), replace(text, suche, ersatz), reverse(liste), round(zahl, stellen?), slice(liste|text, von, bis?), slug(text), some(liste, x => …), sort(liste, (a, b) => …?), sort_by(liste, x => schlüssel), split(text, trenner), sqrt(zahl), starts_with(text, anfang), string(wert), strip_html(text), substr(text, start, länge?), sum(liste), title_case(text), today(), trim(text), unique(liste), upper(text), values(objekt), weekday(datum)

Methods work on values too – strings: toUpperCase, toLowerCase, trim, startsWith, endsWith, split, replace, padStart, padEnd; lists: join, map, filter, find, some, every, reduce, push, pop; numbers: toFixed; any value: includes, slice, indexOf, toString.

Limits (per evaluation): 8 000 characters of source, 4 000 syntax nodes, nesting 60, 20 000 evaluation steps (while (true) {} ends with an error), 150 ms, call depth 40, strings of 100 000 characters, lists of 10 000 items, 200 000 characters of output. Keys named __proto__, constructor and prototype are refused. Regular expressions go through a guard that rejects catastrophic patterns (nested quantifiers) and cuts the input at 10 000 characters. Errors are German messages; in conditions an expression that fails counts as “no”. Syntax is checked when the workflow is saved.

Formatter

Section titled “Formatter”

Text formatieren, Zahl formatieren and Datum / Zeit (format_text, format_number, format_date) apply one operation and store the result.

  • Text: upper/lower/capitalize/title case, trim, collapse spaces, replace, regex replace and extract, split (list, or one piece by position), extract e-mail/phone/number/URL, substring/left/right, length, truncate, pad, slug, URL-encode/decode, prepend/append, default, normalise phone number (+49…), first/last word, initials, reverse, strip HTML, remove diacritics, repeat, line/word count.
  • Number: German and English notation are read (1.234,56, 1,234.56, € 12.50, (3)); round (half up, or banker’s), floor/ceil with decimals, abs, format, currency, percent, pad with zeros, clamp, sign, ordinal, fixed. A lone 1.234 is read as 1.234 (decimal), 1.234.567 as 1 234 567.
  • Date/time: add/subtract minutes, hours, days, weeks, months, years and business days; format with patterns (DD.MM.YYYY HH:mm, dddd, D. MMMM YYYY, shortcuts date, datetime, time, long, iso); convert between time zones; start/end of hour, day, week (Monday), month, year; difference in whole units; weekday; is/next business day; age; ISO and Unix; set the time. Holidays are one YYYY-MM-DD per line.

Time zone rules (all in server/services/formatters.ts, tested in tests/formatters.test.ts):

  • Days, weeks, months, years and business days move the wall clock of the zone: “+1 day” at 12:00 stays 12:00 across a daylight-saving change even though that day has 23 or 25 hours. Minutes and hours are elapsed time.
  • A local time that does not exist (spring gap) moves to the first valid minute; an ambiguous one (autumn) resolves to the later occurrence.
  • Months clamp the day (31 Jan + 1 month = 28/29 Feb); differences count whole calendar units of the zone.
  • The default zone is the sub-account’s time zone.

Flow control

Section titled “Flow control”
Step Notes
Springe zu Schritt (goto) Jumps to any step, optionally under a condition. At most max_jumps jumps (default 10, hard cap 100), then the flow simply continues.
Ziel erreicht → springe (goal_jump) If the goal condition holds: jump to a step or end the flow. Counts as success in split-test statistics.
Split-Test (A/B/n) (split_test) 2–5 paths with percentage weights; each path is a lane of steps, afterwards the flow continues together. “Immer derselbe Pfad” keeps a contact on its path on re-entry. {{var.<name>}} holds the path name. Assignments are stored for the statistics.
Schleife (loop) Runs its steps for each item of a list ({{var.items}}, JSON, or one entry per line). {{var.item}} and {{var.index}} (names configurable). At most 100 items by default, 500 at most; the list is remembered when the loop starts.
Tröpfchen-Freigabe (drip) N contacts per period (for example 20 per hour); a contact that arrives when the slot is full waits for the next one.
Warten bis Bedingung (wait_until) Checks every N minutes (default 15). Optional time limit with continue / end / jump.
Warten auf Zeitfenster (wait_window) Business hours: allowed weekdays, from–to in the zone, holidays skipped. Inside the window the flow continues immediately.
Warten bis Datum (relativ) (wait_date) Waits until a date from the contact, event or a variable plus/minus an offset (minutes … business days) at an optional time; “every year” takes the next anniversary.
Warten auf Antwort (wait_reply) Checks every N minutes for a reply of the contact; {{var.replied}} is yes/no after the wait.

Engine guards: at most 5 000 steps per run (settings.max_steps raises it to 20 000), so no loop or go-to chain runs away; a run that needs many steps carries on in short passes instead of blocking the server. Loops re-enter their lanes; every pass is logged.

Data and CRM steps

Section titled “Data and CRM steps”

Variablen setzen, Mehrere Felder setzen, Felder leeren, Feld kopieren, Liste bearbeiten, JSON auslesen, Wert zuordnen, Zufall, Code (Ausdruck) and Rechnen cover data work. Kontakt suchen / Kontakt suchen oder anlegen find a contact by e-mail, phone or a custom field and can switch the run to the found contact; Deal suchen sets var.deal.*. Deal ändern, Aufgabe ändern/abhaken, Termin anlegen, Termin ändern/absagen, DND je Kanal, Tags je nach Bedingung, Nutzer zuweisen (Round-Robin) (weighted, fair across restarts), Aus allen Workflows entfernen, Interne Benachrichtigung (app, web push, e-mail to staff, SMS to fixed numbers), Eigenes Ereignis auslösen, Rechnung / Angebot erstellen, Zahlungslink erstellen, Vertrag aus Vorlage erstellen, In/Aus Community and Social-Post-Entwurf (mit KI) reuse the existing modules and their rules (permissions, audit log, DND, plan limits).

Per-channel DND is respected when sending from workflows, campaigns and the inbox: single channels (e-mail, SMS) are blocked at send time; “all channels” sets the contact’s general Nicht stören.

AI steps

Section titled “AI steps”

KI: Text einordnen, Felder aus Text lesen, Stimmung erkennen, Zusammenfassen and Übersetzen call the sub-account’s AI (app.services.aiRun) with a JSON schema, so the answer is structured and validated: classify returns one label from your list (or several) plus a confidence, extract fills only fields that are really in the text, sentiment returns positive/neutral/negative and a score, summarise and translate return text. The customer’s text is passed between markers as data; instructions inside it are ignored. All calls count against the monthly AI limit and are logged. Without an AI assistant the step fails with a clear message.

HTTP request

Section titled “HTTP request”

HTTP-Anfrage (Webhook mit Antwort) (http_request): method, URL, query, headers, JSON/form/text body and authentication (bearer, basic or API-key header) with merge fields; up to 5 retries on 429, 5xx and connection errors with exponential back-off (1, 2, 4 … s) that honours Retry-After. The response is available as {{var.response.data.…}} (name configurable); Antwort übernehmen maps single values into variables or contact fields (data.items.0.id, $status, $text, $headers.name). Requests go through safeFetch: internal and private targets are blocked. Header values and passwords are masked in the run log and in exports. Put long-lived keys in Apps → Direkt-Verbindungen rather than in steps.

Native app steps

Section titled “Native app steps”

Twenty-three steps for popular apps, without Composio, built on the connector framework (registerConnector, see connectors.md). Credentials are saved per sub-account under Apps → Direkt-Verbindungen (secrets sealed, never returned to the browser). Where an app signs in with OAuth you can instead enter the name of a Nango integration in the field Nango-Integration; the step then calls the app through Nango’s proxy (see connectors.md). All outbound calls use safeFetch; in the run log tokens, keys and webhook URLs are masked.

Triggers

Section titled “Triggers”

All triggers accept the additional filters below. Triggers that fire from time (Zeitplan, Geburtstag, Jahrestag, Termin beginnt bald, Nachricht unbeantwortet, Rechnung überfällig, Formular abgebrochen) run per workflow in the background scanner and fire once per contact/object (wfx_fired). (Aufgabe überfällig is announced by the planner’s own scan – see below.) These “already fired” marks are kept as long as the scan can still see the record (unanswered messages 10 days, abandoned forms 7, overdue invoices 150, appointments and date triggers about a year), then they are deleted.

Notes on the newer triggers:

  • Feld geändert (field.changed): fires for real changes only – from the contact form, import, API, forms or a workflow. {{event.field}}, {{event.old}}, {{event.new}}; optional from/to values (case-insensitive). A workflow never starts itself with its own changes and chains stop at depth 4.
  • Zeitplan (schedule.cron): five fields m h dom mon dow, lists, ranges, steps, names and macros (@daily). Evaluated on the wall clock of the sub-account’s time zone (or a chosen one): a time inside the spring gap runs right after the gap, a doubled autumn hour fires once; missed ticks are caught up for 10 minutes. At each tick the workflow starts for all contacts matching the tag filters (up to Maximal, at most 5 000); a contact enters only once unless re-entry is allowed.
  • Geburtstag / Jahrestag: once a year per contact at the chosen time in the zone; 29 February counts on 28 February in other years; offset in days before/after; {{event.age}} / {{event.years}}.
  • Missed days (server off) – Geburtstag, Jahrestag and Geburtstag / Datum erreicht (date.reached): a day whose time passed while the server was off (or between two scans around midnight) is caught up at the next scan, at most 3 days back (DATE_CATCH_UP_DAYS, server/services/wfx-triggers.ts); older days are skipped. It never fires twice for the same contact, date and year (the same records as on time: wfx_fired, date_trigger_fires), only for contacts that existed on that day, and only for days after the workflow was last switched on or changed – a workflow switched on today, or paused over the day, does not send it afterwards. The run carries {{event.late_days}} (1–3), so a step can say “nachträglich”.
  • Formular abgebrochen: as soon as an active workflow with this trigger exists, the form remembers name, e-mail and phone while they are typed (nothing else) and starts the workflow when the form is not submitted after the set minutes. Partial entries are deleted after 3 days. Use it only if your form’s privacy notice says so.
  • Seite besucht: recognises the contact by the attribution visitor cookie (set with consent); at most once per contact and page in 30 minutes.
  • Zahlung fehlgeschlagen / erstattet, Abo beendet: come from Stripe webhooks; see Self-hoster setup.
  • Termin nicht wahrgenommen / abgesagt / verschoben / beginnt bald, Nachricht unbeantwortet, Rechnung überfällig: short forms and scanners with a calendar or channel filter and grace times.
  • Rechnung überfällig counts Tage nach Fälligkeit in calendar days of the sub-account’s time zone, like the dunning levels: an invoice due yesterday at 23:00 is 1 day overdue at 08:00 today (the due day itself is 0). {{event.daysOverdue}} counts the same way; 0 days fires as soon as the due time has passed.
  • Aufgabe überfällig, Aufgabe angelegt, Aufgaben-Status geändert, Aufgabe zugewiesen, Aufgabe erledigt: come from the project planner (docs/projects.md); the overdue scan runs there (once per task and due date), and the settings offer a project filter (and status, category or person where it fits). Only tasks with a contact start a workflow. The steps Aufgabe im Projekt anlegen, Aufgabe: Status setzen and Aufgabe zuweisen act on those tasks.
  • Eigenes Ereignis: see Custom events.
  • Bewertung triggers accept min_rating and max_rating.

Outgoing messages

Section titled “Outgoing messages”

Two triggers react to the sending side of a conversation:

  • Ausgehende Nachricht gesendet (message.outbound) – an e-mail, SMS, chat, WhatsApp or other message to the contact went out (sent, delivered, or recorded without a provider; failed sends and drafts do not count, calls have Anruf beendet). Filters: channel and sender – Teammitglied, Kampagne, KI, System (appointment confirmations, invoices …), Workflow. By default messages of workflows are left out, so two automations cannot keep triggering each other; choose Workflow or Alle, auch Workflows to include them. {{event.sender}}, {{event.userName}}, {{event.message.body}} are available.
  • Teammitglied hat geantwortet (message.team_reply) – a person answered the contact (inbox, contact page, app, API/MCP). Filters: channel and person. Typical: stop a reminder sequence, close the conversation, move the deal.

Every path a message takes out (composer, workflows, campaigns, mailbox sync, channel modules) is covered: SQL triggers on the messages table note each message that turns sent in a small queue, which is emptied right after each request and on every tick.

Trigger filters

Section titled “Trigger filters”

Every trigger has Zusätzliche Filter in the editor, stored in the trigger config:

field is a contact field (tag, email, first_name, or contact.custom.<key> for a custom field), event.… or var.…; filters_match is all or any; filter_expression is an expression and wins over the list when set.

Condition operators (also used by Wenn/Sonst, Springe zu, Warten bis …): has, not_has, equals, not_equals, contains, starts_with, ends_with, in, not_in (comma list), empty, not_empty, gt, gte, lt, lte, between (10, 20), regex (guarded), date_before, date_after (now or a date).

Test runs

Section titled “Test runs”

Testlauf in the editor (or POST /api/workflows/:id/test-run, MCP ghl_workflow_test_run) plays the workflow through with sample data without sending or changing anything:

  • Steps that only compute (formatter, math, code, variables, find, flow control, loops, split test, waits as “would wait until …”) really run; their results appear in the log and in the variables at the end.
  • Steps with side effects (messages, writes, API calls, AI) show what they would do: the rendered inputs, marked “nicht ausgeführt”.
  • Sample contact (Erika Muster) or a real contact that is only read; the event comes from the trigger’s sample (editable JSON), plus optional preset variables. Unsaved changes can be tested without saving (workflow: { trigger, steps, settings }).
  • Waits end the run with the status “würde warten”; the step cap and loop caps apply.

Run log

Section titled “Run log”

Verlauf → Protokoll shows every step of a run with its input, output, the values it set and the time in milliseconds (GET /api/workflow-runs/:id). Secrets (tokens, keys, passwords, webhook URLs, authorization headers) are masked, long values are clipped. The run list itself carries only a slim log.

Error handling

Section titled “Error handling”

What happens when a step fails (Einstellungen of the workflow and the step’s own editor):

  • Per step – “Fehler ignorieren und weiter” (continue_on_error: true): the error is written to the run log (marked as ignored), the run goes on with the next step – also when the workflow stops on errors – and nothing is reported.
  • Per workflow – “Bei Fehler anhalten” (stop_on_error) as before: the run stops at the failing step with the status fehlgeschlagen; without it, it logs the error and continues with the next step.
  • Per workflow – reaction (on_error_workflow_id, error_bell, error_email), once per run for the first failing step that is not ignored (and again after a repeat that fails again):
    • “Bei Fehler: anderen Workflow starten” runs the chosen (active) workflow for the same contact. It sees the error as {{event.error.message}}, {{event.error.step}} (the step’s name as in the editor), {{event.error.workflow}}, {{event.error.step_type}}, {{event.error.run_id}} and {{event.error.stopped}}; its trigger should be Manuell. A run that is itself an error handler never starts another one (no loops). The workflow can only be one of the same sub-account (copies of a snapshot point at the copied handler, imports drop the setting).
    • Glocke sends a push notification (kind Workflow-Fehler, switchable under Mein Konto) to everyone with access to the area Automationen; E-Mail goes to the company e-mail with the failing step, the message and the contact. At most 10 notices per workflow and hour.
  • Repeat from the failed step: a stopped run offers Ab Fehler wiederholen in Verlauf (and Alle ab dem Fehler wiederholen for the whole workflow): it continues at the step that failed – the steps before it do not run again – after you fixed the step or its cause. POST /api/workflow-runs/:id/retry, POST /api/workflows/:id/retry-failed (at most 100, oldest first). Needs an active workflow; the step budget starts fresh. Runs that continued after an error (no stop on error) cannot be repeated – their later steps already ran.
  • Outgoing webhooks and REST hooks get the event workflow.step_failed {contactId, failure: {workflowId, workflowName, runId, stepId, stepType, step, message, stopped, at}} for workflows with a reaction set up. Test runs report nothing.

Versions, draft and publish

Section titled “Versions, draft and publish”
  • Every saved change (editor, API, MCP, import) creates a version (hash-deduplicated, the last 50 are kept) with the user and a short summary (“2 Schritte neu · 1 geändert”).
  • Entwurf: Versionen → Aktuellen Stand als Entwurf speichern stores the editor state without touching the live workflow (PUT /api/workflows/:id/draft). Entwurf veröffentlichen (POST …/publish, optional note) replaces the workflow and becomes a version. Als Entwurf wiederherstellen puts an old version into the draft; nothing goes live until you publish.
  • Saving in the editor stays immediate, as before – the draft is for bigger rebuilds that you test first.

Custom events

Section titled “Custom events”

POST /api/custom-events with { "name": "bestellung_versendet", "email": "a@b.de", "create": true, "data": { … } } (contact by contact_id, email or phone; create makes the contact if needed; data up to 50 KB) fires the trigger Eigenes Ereignis (name empty = any). The data is {{event.<field>}}. The same works from MCP (ghl_emit_event) and from the step Eigenes Ereignis auslösen. Chains are limited to 4 levels and a workflow never starts itself.

Limits and safety

Section titled “Limits and safety”
  • Expression evaluator: see Expression language. Regular expressions are guarded everywhere (text steps, conditions, form patterns of apps).
  • Per run: 5 000 steps (raise with settings.max_steps to 20 000), loops 100/500 items, go-to 10/100 jumps, HTTP retries 5.
  • The loop and go-to guards do not throw – they continue after the limit and say so in the log.
  • Native app steps validate every host (Shopify *.myshopify.com, Mailchimp data center, Teams webhook hosts, https only for WooCommerce) and use safeFetch; the Stripe steps use the sub-account’s own key with idempotency keys.
  • Secrets never reach the browser, the run log or exports.

Adding your own step

Section titled “Adding your own step”

Modules register steps through the hooks on app.services:

  • workflowActions[type] = (run, step, vars, flowCtx) => string | ActionOutcome – the handler.
  • workflowActionMeta[type] – the description for the picker and the generated form (ActionMeta: label, category, summary, doc, fields, provides, pure (allowed in test runs), needs, app, sample).
  • workflowTriggers (type names), workflowTriggerMeta[type] (TriggerMeta with fields and a sample event) and workflowTriggerFilters[type] = (config, data) => boolean.
  • workflowStepCleaners[type] (and '*') – normalise or reject a step when a workflow is saved.
  • workflowStepSkips – a list of (step) => reason | undefined; a reason makes the engine skip the step with that log line (used by pausing).
  • registerActions / registerActionMeta / registerTriggers in server/services/wfx-kit.ts do the bookkeeping.

An ActionOutcome may carry result (log line), output, vars, fields (contact fields to write), goto, enter (a lane of a container), wait_until, stay, end and contact_id (switch contact). The code is split in server/services/wfx-*.ts (data, flow, AI, HTTP, catalogue, triggers helpers) and server/modules/wfx-*.ts, connector-apps-*.ts; none of it lives in main.ts, server.ts, the state store or protocol.ts.

What the tests check for you. tests/wfx-counts.test.ts fails when a registered step or trigger is missing from the catalogue (or has no label, summary or category). tests/wfx-sweep.test.ts walks the catalogue: it fires the event of every trigger (with its sample) and expects a run, and runs every action with its sample settings behind the outbound guard, expecting a result or a readable German error – never a JavaScript error name or a stack frame. A new step that has no sample is run with empty settings, so give it one. What a step actually does still needs its own test.
