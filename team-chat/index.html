Team-Chat – talk to your team inside HyperLevel

The Team-Chat is the internal messenger of a sub-account (and, if you want, of the whole agency): channels (public or private), direct messages and small groups, threads, emoji reactions, @mentions, files, pinned messages, search, typing indicators and presence. It is wired into the CRM: pasting a link to a contact or ticket shows a live card, /aufgabe creates a task, /kontakt shares a contact, /agent asks the HyperLevel Agent in the channel. Notifications arrive as in-app counters, web push and on the Android app.

Menu: Team-Chat (/app/team-chat, area teamchat in the member rights) with an unread badge. No extra service, no npm package, nothing to install: the realtime part is one Server-Sent-Events stream per open tab (SSE for what the server tells you, ordinary POST requests for everything you do).

It does not replace the customer conversations (Konversationen – that is the inbox for customers) and it does not duplicate other pieces:

Existing piece How the chat uses it
Tasks /aufgabe calls the task route as you (same rights, same audit log); chat cards show the task live.
Contacts, companies, tickets, Canvas, Wiki Cards in messages (pasted links to /app/contacts/…, /app/tickets/… etc., or the share button); a message can be saved as an internal note on a contact (… → Als Notiz an einem Kontakt speichern).
Slack bridge Optional one-way copy of a channel to the Slack channel set up under Einstellungen → Weitere Kanäle → Slack (see below).
Web push, Android feed Push kind chat, feed event type chat – see notifications.
HyperLevel Agent /agent runs the agent with your rights; anything it wants to change waits for your click.

Using it

Section titled “Using it”
Do How
Send Enter sends, Shift + Enter starts a new line. A message is Markdown-light: **bold**, *italic*, `code`, code blocks, lists, quotes, links.
Mention @ and a name (or the picker); @alle (also @channel, @here) addresses everybody in the channel.
Reply in a thread Hover a message → ↳. Replies stay together; the channel shows “3 Antworten”. Threads are one level deep.
React Hover → 😀＋, or click an existing reaction. One reaction per person and emoji.
Pin … → Anpinnen (up to 100 per channel); the pin button in the header lists them.
Edit / delete … → Bearbeiten (own messages) / Löschen (own; channel owners and – in public channels – agency admins for others). A deleted message leaves a “gelöscht” line, its text, files and reactions are gone.
Files Paperclip, paste or drop (up to 10 per message, 8 MB each; PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, PDF, text/CSV, Office, ZIP – SVG/HTML refused). Images show inline.
Search The field above the channel list: finds messages in every channel and conversation you may read; a hit jumps to the message.
Unread Bold names and counters (red = you were mentioned); a “Neu” line marks where you stopped reading. The menu entry shows the total of channels that are not muted.
Mute / loudness Bell in the header per channel: Alle Nachrichten, Nur Erwähnungen, Stumm.

Channels, direct messages and who can read what

Section titled “Channels, direct messages and who can read what”
Kind Who can read and join
Public channel Everyone with the teamchat right in that sub-account can read it and joins by writing (or the Kanal beitreten button).
Private channel Invited members only. Agency admins cannot read it either – it is invisible to everybody else (a request answers “not found”).
Agency-wide channel (admins create it, Gilt für: Die ganze Agentur) Shows up in every sub-account for agency admins (public ones readable, join by writing) and for members who were invited; nobody else sees it.
Direct message / group (up to 8 people) The participants only – not even agency admins. They belong to the person, not to a sub-account.

Rules worth knowing: a channel owner (the creator) renames, sets the topic, archives, invites to private channels and deletes; archived channels stay readable. Mentioning someone who is not in a private channel does nothing (the chat never reveals who is outside). People who lose the teamchat right or access to the sub-account lose the channel at once, including their open stream. A stream also ends with the login it was opened with: signing out, “sign out other devices”, a revoked session or a new password end it at once, an expired session within 20 seconds (the browser is told bye and tries again only when the tab is looked at again).

Commands

Section titled “Commands”
Command What happens
/aufgabe Angebot senden morgen @Anna Creates a task. The due day understands heute, morgen, übermorgen, Montag … Sonntag, 15.10., 15.10.2026, 2026-10-15; the first @mention becomes the assignee. The channel gets “📌 Aufgabe angelegt” with a live card. Needs the contacts right (tasks live there).
/kontakt Müller Searches contacts; one hit is posted as a card, several offer a small chooser.
/agent Wie viele Aufgaben sind überfällig? Posts your question and the agent’s answer in the channel – everybody there reads it. The agent sees the last ten messages of that channel as context (as data, not as instructions) and has your rights. It reads freely; if it wants to change something (create a deal, send a message …) the message shows “Der Agent möchte etwas ändern” with Ausführen / Ablehnen buttons – only the person who asked can confirm, once, for exactly those arguments. The mode “automatic” of the agent settings is never used here: the most it can do is “ask”.

Commands run with the caller’s own rights through the normal routes, so they appear in the audit log like everything else. The agent needs the HyperLevel Agent to be switched on for the sub-account and a text model (Einstellungen → KI-Agent, KI-Anbieter); otherwise the command answers with that reason immediately.

Notifications

Section titled “Notifications”
Where When
In the app live over the stream: new messages, unread counters, typing (“Anna tippt …”), who is online
Web push (kind chat, switch under Einstellungen → Mein Konto) direct messages and groups: every message; channels: when you are @mentioned or someone replies in a thread you took part in – or every message if you chose Alle Nachrichten for that channel. Never for muted channels, never for your own messages, never while you are looking at the chat (a tab in the foreground counts for 45 seconds after its last signal)
Android app (feed event type chat, category Team-Chat) the same people and rules; tapping opens the channel

Push messages carry a short text preview (160 characters); people who lost access in between are skipped.

Slack copy (optional)

Section titled “Slack copy (optional)”

Kanal-Einstellungen → “Neue Nachrichten in Slack spiegeln” (visible when the Slack bridge of the sub-account is set up and enabled; works with the bot token and with an incoming webhook). Every new message of that channel is posted to the one Slack channel the bridge uses, as “Name in #channel: text” – plain text, 160 characters of preview, no files. It is one way: Slack replies do not come back, and agency-wide channels cannot be mirrored. Private channels can be mirrored by their owner – think before you do, Slack’s members then read them.

Retention

Section titled “Retention”

Team-Chat → + → Chat-Einstellungen (needs the right Einstellungen): keep messages unlimited (default), or delete everything older than 30, 90, 180 or 365 days, with their files; pinned messages stay; a thread is removed together with its first message. Agency admins set the value for agency-wide channels separately. The cleanup runs every 30 minutes in the background tick and writes no per-message audit lines (the setting itself is audited). Deleting a channel removes its messages and files for good.

Realtime and self-hosting

Section titled “Realtime and self-hosting”
  • One HyperLevel process serves the streams (an in-memory hub); a restart makes the browsers reconnect by themselves (EventSource retry 3 s) and the chat reloads what it missed. Capacity: 600 open streams, 8 per person; a full server answers 503 (the tab tries again when it is looked at again – no reconnect every 3 s); a heartbeat every 20 s keeps proxies from closing idle streams and drops dead ones.
  • Reverse proxy: the stream must not be buffered. HyperLevel sends X-Accel-Buffering: no and Cache-Control: no-transform; with nginx also set proxy_read_timeout generously (the heartbeat is 20 s) and never enable response buffering for /api/team-chat/stream. If the stream is blocked, the chat still works – messages appear after you send or reload – but not live.
  • Files are stored under data/teamchat-files (random names, served only through the authenticated route with nosniff and a locked-down content-security-policy); the backup covers the folder. The demo mode empties it on reset.
  • Rate limit: 20 messages per person per 10 seconds; messages up to 8,000 characters.
  • Search is SQLite FTS5 with a LIKE fallback, joined against the message table so that sub-account and access rules always apply.
  • Privacy: messages are not sent anywhere except the optional Slack copy, push previews (through the browser push services, text only) and the agent if somebody types /agent. They are part of your own backups (database and data/teamchat-files); direct messages and private channels are not visible to agency admins in the app.

MCP, API and mobile

Section titled “MCP, API and mobile”
  • MCP: ghl_chat_post and ghl_chat_history – public channels of one sub-account only; keys and MCP connections never see private channels or direct messages. Posts show up as a bot (“KI-App”).
  • Mobile (device token): /api/mobile/chat/… for the channel list, messages, posting and reading.
